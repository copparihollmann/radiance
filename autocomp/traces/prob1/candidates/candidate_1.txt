On RISC-V targets, integer division and modulo operations are extremely slow. Because the dimensions (`KH`, `KW`, `OW`, `CKK`) are runtime variables passed via the argument struct, the compiler cannot optimize them to bitwise shifts or masks. These dynamic divisions occur inside the inner loop for every single loaded element, creating a massive compute bottleneck.
